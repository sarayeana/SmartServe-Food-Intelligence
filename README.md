# SmartServe Food Intelligence

## Project Overview

SmartServe Food Intelligence is an end-to-end data analytics and machine learning project designed to address a real-world food service problem: improving demand planning, reducing food waste, optimizing inventory, and supporting data-driven business decisions.

The project combines SQL, Python, machine Learning, Excel, and Power BI to build a complete decision-support solution for a fictional multi-store food service business.

The solution will analyze historical sales and operational data, identify the drivers of demand and food waste, forecast future demand, assess waste risk, and generate inventory and preparation recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Food service businesses frequently face two competing operational challenges:

* Over-preparation can lead to excess inventory and food waste.
* Under-preparation can result in stockouts, lost sales, and poor customer experience.

Store managers must make daily preparation and inventory decisions while dealing with changing customer demand, promotions, holidays, seasonal patterns, weather conditions, and differences between store locations.

Without accurate demand forecasting and operational insights, businesses may lose revenue through both avoidable food waste and missed sales opportunities.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an integrated food service intelligence solution that helps SmartServe:

* Analyze sales and operational performance.
* Understand the drivers of customer demand.
* Identify the causes and cost of food waste.
* Detect stockout and lost-sales opportunities.
* Evaluate promotion performance.
* Forecast future product demand.
* Predict food waste risk.
* Recommend inventory and preparation quantities.
* Estimate the potential financial impact of operational improvements.
